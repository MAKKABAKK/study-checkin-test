## Work / Study · 工作与学习

原讲义第 4–6 页

### 1. Are you a student or a worker?

**题意** 你现在是学生，还是已经工作了？

> 原讲义用这道题来分流。已经工作的同学接着练工作类问题，在读的同学可以直接跳到学生类问题。

**已工作的追问**

### 2. What work do you do?

**题意** 你做什么工作？

I work as a xxx at a tech company. My job involves writing code and solving problems for our clients.

**参考译文** 我在一家科技公司做 xxx。我的工作包括写代码，也包括帮客户解决问题。

### 3. Why did you choose to do that type of work (or that job)?

**题意** 为什么选择这类工作（或这份工作）？

I chose this field because I've always been interested in xxx. The job offers good opportunities for growth and learning new skills. It also pays relatively well compared to other jobs.

**参考译文** 我选这个行业，是因为我一直对 xxx 很感兴趣。这份工作给了我成长和学习新技能的机会，而且跟其他工作比，收入也挺不错。

### 4. Do you like your job?

**题意** 你喜欢自己的工作吗？

Yes, I enjoy my job most days. Creating something that people actually use feels rewarding. My colleagues are supportive, and the work environment is quite pleasant.

**参考译文** 大多数时候都挺喜欢的。看到自己做出来的东西真的有人用，我很有成就感。同事们也很支持我，工作氛围挺好。

### 5. What requirements did you need to meet to get your current job?

**题意** 要得到现在这份工作，你当时得满足哪些条件？

I needed a degree in college，problem-solving skills and some previous project experience, even small ones.

**参考译文** 我得有大学学位，要会解决问题，还要有做项目的经验，哪怕只是小项目也行。

### 6. Do you have any plans for your work in the next five years?

**题意** 未来五年，你在工作上有什么打算？

I hope to advance to a senior position and maybe lead a small team. I'm also interested in learning more in AI if possible.

**参考译文** 我希望能升到更资深的职位，也许还能带一个小团队。有机会的话，我还想多学点人工智能方面的东西。

### 7. What do you think is the most important at the moment?

**题意** 你觉得现阶段最重要的是什么？

Learning new technologies is most important right now. The world changes quickly, and staying updated with the latest tools and methods is important.

**参考译文** 我现在觉得学新技术最重要。世界变化得很快，跟上最新的工具和方法很重要。

### 8. Do you want to change to another job?

**题意** 你想换工作吗？

Not right now as I'm still learning a lot in my current position. Maybe in a few years I might look for new challenges at a different company or industry.

**参考译文** 暂时不想，我在现在的岗位上还能学到很多东西。也许过几年，我会去别的公司或行业，找些新的挑战。

### 9. What technology do you use at work?

**题意** 你工作时会用到哪些技术？

I use various softwares, and testing tools. We also have wechat for team communication.

**参考译文** 我会用各种软件和测试工具。我们团队平时也用微信沟通。

### 10. Who helps you the most? And how?

**题意** 谁对你的帮助最大？是怎么帮你的？

My team leader helps me the most with guidance and career advice. He gives constructive feedback on my work and suggests resources for improving my skills.

**参考译文** 我的组长帮我最多。他会指导我，给我职业发展方面的建议，也会对我的工作提出建设性的意见，推荐能帮我提升技能的学习资源。

### 11. What are the benefits of being your age?

**题意** 处在你现在这个年龄，有什么好处？

At my age, I have enough experience to be competent but I'm still young enough to adapt to new technologies.

**参考译文** 以我现在的年龄，经验已经足以让我胜任工作，同时我还很年轻，适应新技术也比较容易。

**在读学生的追问**

### 12. What subjects are you studying?

**题意** 你现在在学哪些科目？

I'm studying data science at university now. It's quite challenging but very interesting. We learn programming and how computers work.

**参考译文** 我现在在大学读数据科学。这个专业挺有挑战性，不过也很有意思。我们学编程，也学计算机的工作原理。

### 13. Do you like your subject?

**题意** 你喜欢自己的专业吗？

Yes, I really enjoy it. Data science is fascinating and has good job prospects. I especially like the practical projects we do.

**参考译文** 喜欢，我真的挺享受学习的过程。数据科学很有意思，就业前景也不错。我尤其喜欢我们做的那些实践项目。

### 14. Why did you choose to study that subject?

**题意** 你为什么选择这个专业？

I've always been interested in conputer since I was young. Also my parents work in that field.

**参考译文** 我从小就对计算机感兴趣，而且我父母也在这个领域工作。

> 拼写提示：原文的 conputer 指 computer（计算机）。这里表达“对计算机感兴趣”时，可以说 interested in computers。

### 15. Do you think that your subject is popular in your country?

**题意** 你觉得这个专业在你的国家受欢迎吗？

Yes, Data science is very popular now. Many students choose it because it pays well.

**参考译文** 挺受欢迎的，现在数据科学很热门。很多学生选这个专业，是因为相关工作的薪水不错。

### 16. Do you have any plans for your studies in the next five years?

**题意** 你对未来五年的学业有什么计划？

I want to finish my degree and maybe get some work experience. Later, I might study for a master's degree. I need to improve my skills to get a good job.

**参考译文** 我想先拿到学位，可能也会积累一些工作经验。之后也许会读硕士。想找到好工作，还得继续提高自己的能力。

### 17. What are the benefits of being your age?

**题意** 处在你现在这个年龄，有什么好处？

Being young means I have energy and time to learn new things. I can try different paths before settling down. Also, I don't have many responsibilities yet.

**参考译文** 年轻让我有精力，也有时间学新东西。在决定以后的方向之前，我可以多试几条路。而且我现在要承担的责任还没那么多。

### 18. Do you want to change your major?

**题意** 你想换专业吗？

No, I'm happy with my major. It suits my interests and abilities. The job opportunities are good, so I'll stick with it.

**参考译文** 不想，我挺喜欢现在的专业。它既符合我的兴趣，也适合我的能力。就业机会也不错，所以我打算继续读下去。

### 19. Do you prefer to study in the mornings or in the afternoons?

**题意** 你更喜欢上午学习还是下午学习？

I prefer mornings when my mind is fresh. I can focus better and understand difficult concepts. After lunch, I usually feel a bit tired.

**参考译文** 我更喜欢上午，那时头脑比较清醒，更容易集中注意力，也更容易理解难点。吃过午饭以后，我通常会有点累。

### 20. How much time do you spend on your studies each week?

**题意** 你每周花多少时间学习？

About 30 hours including classes and self-study. During exam periods, I spend much more time, maybe 50 hours. It depends on the assignments.

**参考译文** 上课和自学加在一起大概 30 个小时。考试期间会长很多，有时能到 50 个小时，具体还得看作业量。

### 21. Are you looking forward to working?

**题意** 你期待参加工作吗？

Yes, I'm excited about getting a real job. I want to apply what I've learned and earn my own money. Working on actual projects will be interesting.

**参考译文** 期待，我挺向往真正开始工作的。我想把学到的东西用起来，自己挣钱。能参与实际项目，应该会很有意思。

### 22. What technology do you use when you study?

**题意** 你学习时会用到哪些技术？

I mainly use my laptop for programming and research. I also use online learning platforms and digital textbooks. Sometimes I watch video tutorials too.

**参考译文** 我主要用笔记本电脑编程和做研究，也会用在线学习平台和电子教材，有时还会看教学视频。

### 23. What changes would you like to see in your school?

**题意** 你希望学校做出哪些改变？

I wish we had more practical projects and less theory. Also, newer computers in the labs would be nice. More connections with companies for internships would help.

**参考译文** 我希望能多做些实践项目，理论课稍微少一点。实验室如果能换些新电脑就好了。学校也可以多和企业合作，给学生提供实习机会。

## Home/accommodation · 住房

原讲义第 6–8 页

### 1. What kind of house or apartment do you want to live in in the future?

**题意** 你将来想住在什么样的房子或公寓里？

I'd like to have a small apartment with good natural light and a balcony for some plants. It should be in a quiet area but not too far from the city center. Two bedrooms would be enough for me.

**参考译文** 我想住在一套小公寓里，自然采光好，有个阳台可以种点花草。周围最好安静一些，离市中心又别太远。对我来说，两间卧室就够了。

### 2. Are the transport facilities to your home very good?

**题意** 你家附近的交通方便吗？

Yes, transportation is quite convenient where I live. There's a bus stop just five minutes away, and the subway station is about a 15-minute walk. It makes commuting to university relatively easy.

**参考译文** 挺方便的。从我家走到公交站只要五分钟，走到地铁站大概一刻钟，所以平时去大学上课还算方便。

### 3. Do you prefer living in a house or an apartment?

**题意** 你更喜欢住房子还是住公寓？

I prefer apartments for now because they're easier to maintain and usually have better security. Houses require more cleaning and maintenance work. Maybe when I'm older with a family, I'll want a house.

**参考译文** 现阶段我更喜欢公寓，打理起来更省事，安保条件通常也更好。住在房子里，清洁和维护要花更多精力。也许等我年纪大些、有了自己的家庭，就会更想要一栋房子。

### 4. Please describe the room you live in.

**题意** 请描述一下你住的房间。

My bedroom is small but comfortable, with a single bed, a desk for studying, and a wardrobe. I have some photos on the wall and a small bookshelf. The window faces east, so I get nice morning sunlight.

**参考译文** 我的卧室不大，但挺舒服。有一张单人床、一张学习用的书桌和一个衣柜。我在墙上挂了几张照片，还有一个小书架。窗户朝东，早上的阳光很好。

### 5. What part of your home do you like the most?

**题意** 你最喜欢家里的哪个地方？

I like our living room the most because it's bright and spacious. We have a comfortable sofa where I can relax, read books, or watch movies. It's also where family members gather to talk about our day.

**参考译文** 我最喜欢客厅，因为它既明亮又宽敞。客厅里有张舒服的沙发，我可以坐在那儿放松、看书或看电影。我们一家人也常聚在那里，聊聊一天里发生的事。

### 6. How long have you lived there?

**题意** 你在那里住多久了？

I've lived in this apartment for about three years now. Before that, we lived in another district that was further from the city center. This location is much more convenient for daily life.

**参考译文** 我在这套公寓住了大约三年。以前我们住在另一个区，离市中心更远。现在这里日常生活方便多了。

### 7. Do you plan to live there for a long time?

**题意** 你打算在那里长住吗？

Probably not very long. After I graduate, I'll likely move to wherever I find a job. I see my current place as temporary while I complete my studies at university.

**参考译文** 应该不会住特别久。我毕业后大概会搬到找到工作的地方。现在的住处算是我读大学期间的临时落脚点。

### 8. What's the difference between where you are living now and where you have lived in the past?

**题意** 你现在住的地方和以前住的地方有什么不同？

My current place is smaller but in a better location than our previous home. The old place had more rooms but was far from good transport links. This neighborhood has more shops and restaurants nearby.

**参考译文** 现在的住处比以前小，不过位置更好。以前的房子房间更多，但搭公共交通不太方便。现在家附近有更多商店和餐馆。

### 9. Can you describe the place where you live?

**题意** 你能描述一下你住的地方吗？

I live in a medium-sized apartment in a residential building with about 20 floors. Our unit is on the 8th floor, so we have a nice view of the neighborhood. The building is about 10 years old and has a small garden area.

**参考译文** 我住在一栋大约 20 层的住宅楼里，公寓大小适中。我家在八楼，能看到不错的街区景色。这栋楼大约有十年了，还有一小片花园。

### 10. What room does your family spend most of the time in?

**题意** 你们一家人平时在哪个房间待得最久？

Definitely the living room. It's where we eat together, watch TV, and chat about our day. On weekends, we often spend hours there just relaxing or entertaining guests who come to visit.

**参考译文** 肯定是客厅。我们在那里一起吃饭、看电视，聊聊各自一天的经历。周末常常一待就是好几个小时，有时单纯放松，有时招待来家里的客人。

### 11. What's your favorite room in your apartment or house?

**题意** 你家里最喜欢的房间是哪个？

My favorite is probably the small balcony connected to our living room. It's not technically a room, but I've put some plants and a small chair there. It's peaceful to sit there with a cup of tea.

**参考译文** 我最喜欢的大概是和客厅相连的小阳台。严格来说它算不上一个房间，不过我在那儿摆了几盆植物和一把小椅子。端杯茶坐在那里，特别安静惬意。

### 12. What makes you feel pleasant in your home?

**题意** 家里的哪些方面让你觉得舒服？

The comfort of familiar things and the sense of privacy make me feel good at home. I also appreciate having my own space to relax without worrying about how I look or act. Home is where I can truly be myself.

**参考译文** 家里熟悉的东西让我安心，有自己的私人空间也让我觉得舒服。我可以放松下来，不用在意自己的样子或举止。在家里我可以做最真实的自己。

### 13. Do you think it is important to live in a comfortable environment?

**题意** 你觉得住得舒适重要吗？

Yes, very important. Where you live affects your mood and productivity every day. A comfortable home provides a place to rest properly and reduces stress. It's worth investing time to make your living space pleasant.

**参考译文** 很重要。住在哪里，每天都会影响你的心情和做事的效率。舒服的家能让人好好休息，也有助于减轻压力。花点时间让住处变得舒心是值得的。

### 14. Do you live in an apartment or a house?

**题意** 你住的是公寓，还是一栋房子？

I live in an apartment with my family. It's a common place in the city due to limited space and high property prices. Most of my friends also live in apartments rather than houses.

**参考译文** 我和家人一起住公寓。城市里空间有限，房价也高，住公寓很常见。我的大多数朋友住的也是公寓。

### 15. Who do you live with?

**题意** 你和谁一起住？

I live with my parents. Many young people in my country live with family during university to save money. We get along well most of the time, though sometimes I wish for more privacy.

**参考译文** 我和父母一起住。在我们国家，很多年轻人上大学时为了省钱会和家人同住。我们大部分时候相处得不错，不过有时我也希望能多一点私人空间。

### 16. What do you usually do in your apartment?

**题意** 你通常在公寓里做些什么？

Besides sleeping, I study at my desk, help with cooking sometimes, and relax by watching shows or reading. On weekends, I might invite a friend over to study together or just chat in the living room.

**参考译文** 除了睡觉，我会在书桌前学习，有时帮忙做饭，也会看看剧、读读书，放松一下。周末可能会请朋友来一起学习，或者就在客厅聊聊天。

### 17. What kinds of accommodation do you live in?

**题意** 你住的是什么类型的房子？

I live in a standard three-bedroom apartment in a residential complex. It's nothing fancy but comfortable enough for our needs. The building has an elevator and basic security at the entrance.

**参考译文** 我住在一个住宅小区里，是套普通的三居室。房子算不上豪华，但我们一家住得挺舒服。楼里有电梯，入口也有基本的安保措施。

## Hometown · 家乡

原讲义第 9–10 页

### 1. Where is your hometown?

**题意** 你的家乡在哪里？

My hometown is a city in eastern/western/northern/southern China. It's located near the coast/mountain/river and has beautiful scenery. The weather there is quite mild compared to other parts of the country.

**参考译文** 我的家乡在中国东部／西部／北部／南部的一座城市。它靠近海边／山区／河流，风景很美。跟国内其他地方相比，那里的气候比较温和。

### 2. Is that a big city or a small place?

**题意** 那是一座大城市，还是一个小地方？

It's a medium-sized city by Chinese standards. The population is around a few million people, which is considered moderate in China.

**参考译文** 按中国的标准，算一座中等城市。人口大约有几百万，在国内算中等规模。

### 3. Please describe your hometown a little.

**题意** 请简单描述一下你的家乡。

My hometown is quite a beautiful place with a mix of modern buildings and some traditional architecture. There are several parks where people like to gather. The food is famous for being slightly sweet/sour/hot compared to other Chinese cuisines.

**参考译文** 我的家乡很漂亮，既有现代建筑，也保留了一些传统建筑。有好几座公园，大家喜欢去那里聚一聚。和中国其他地方的菜相比，我们那里的菜以口味偏甜／偏酸／偏辣而闻名。

### 4. How long have you been living there?

**题意** 你在那里生活了多久？

I lived there for about 18 years before moving away for university. I grew up there and completed all my school education in my hometown. Most of my childhood memories are connected to that place.

**参考译文** 我在那里住了大约 18 年，后来才去外地上大学。我在那里长大，上大学前一直在家乡读书。我大部分的童年回忆都和那个地方有关。

### 5. Do you think you will continue living there for a long time?

My morning routine starts with waking up at 6:30. I do some stretches, then take a shower and get dressed. I have breakfast, usually something light like congee or bread with milk. Then I pack my bag, check I have everything, and head out by 7:45.

**参考译文** 我每天六点半起床，先做几个拉伸动作，再洗澡、穿衣服。然后吃早餐，一般吃点清淡的，比如粥，或者面包配牛奶。接着收拾好包，检查东西都带齐了，最晚七点四十五分出门。

### 4. Do you like to get up early in the morning?

**题意** 你喜欢早起吗？

I don't naturally enjoy getting up early, but I've trained myself to do it. Early mornings are peaceful and allow me to start the day without rushing. It took some time to adjust, but now I appreciate the quiet morning hours.

**参考译文** 我本来并不喜欢早起，不过已经慢慢练成习惯了。清晨很安静，我可以从容地开始新的一天，不用着急忙慌。刚开始花了一段时间适应，现在我很享受早晨安静的这段时光。
