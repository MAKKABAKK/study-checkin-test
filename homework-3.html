作业三 · 口语背诵

住房六问

每题用 2–4 句回答，六题说完算一轮。作业要求至少练 30 轮，熟练后录音。

这组参考回答以「和朋友合租两居室」为例。先把室友、房间和日常活动换成自己的情况，再合上答案练习。

  1. What do you usually do in your flat?

    参考回答与记忆提示

    On weekdays, I usually study at my desk and cook something simple for dinner. After dinner, my flatmate and I often watch a show together.

    书桌前学习 → 做顿简单的晚饭 → 和室友看剧

  2. Do you plan to live there for a long time?

    参考回答与记忆提示

    I’ll probably stay here until I finish my studies. After that, I’d like to find a place closer to work so I can spend less time on the bus.

    住到毕业 → 搬到公司附近 → 少坐公交

  3. What kind of accommodation do you live in?

    参考回答与记忆提示

    I share a two-bedroom flat with a friend. We each have our own bedroom, but we share the kitchen and the living room.

    和朋友合租两居室 → 卧室各自用 → 厨房、客厅共用

  4. Can you describe the place where you live?

    参考回答与记忆提示

    It’s a small flat on the third floor. The living room gets a lot of sunlight, and from the sofa I can see the park across the road.

    三楼的小公寓 → 客厅采光好 → 坐在沙发上能看见公园

  5. What’s your favourite room in your home?

    参考回答与记忆提示

    My favourite room is my bedroom because it’s the quietest part of the flat. My desk is right by the window, so I can look outside when I take a break.

    卧室最安静 → 书桌靠窗 → 休息时看看窗外

  6. Is there anything you would like to change about your home?

    参考回答与记忆提示

    I’d like to get a bigger kitchen table. The one we have is fine for two people, but when our friends come over for dinner, we run out of space for the dishes.

    想换大一点的餐桌 → 两人够用 → 朋友来时放不下菜
